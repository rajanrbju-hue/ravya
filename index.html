Ravya Enterprises – Solar Energy Company

Ravya Enterprises is one of the leading Solar EPC companies in Motihari, East Champaran, providing complete rooftop solar solutions for homes, businesses, schools, hospitals, industries, and commercial establishments.

We specialize in on-grid, off-grid, and hybrid solar systems with professional installation, net metering assistance, government subsidy support, annual maintenance, and project financing.

We offer premium solar panels, inverters, and batteries from trusted brands like UTL and TATA. Serving Motihari, Chakia, Raxaul, Sugauli, Kesariya, Piprakothi, Mehsi, Turkaulia, and the entire East Champaran district.

Our Services

Complete Solar Solutions

Residential Solar

Custom rooftop solar systems for homes. Reduce electricity bills and enjoy clean energy with government subsidy benefits.

Commercial Solar

Solar solutions for offices, shops, schools, hospitals and commercial buildings. Maximize savings and sustainability.

Industrial Solar

Large-scale solar power plants for industries. Reliable power, reduced operational costs and green credentials.

On-Grid Systems

Grid-tied solar systems with net metering. Export excess power and earn credits on your electricity bill.

Off-Grid & Hybrid

Battery-backed systems for uninterrupted power. Ideal for areas with frequent outages or no grid access.

AMC & Maintenance

Annual maintenance contracts, cleaning, performance monitoring and repairs to keep your system running optimally.
